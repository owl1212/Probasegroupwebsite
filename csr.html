Corporate Social Responsibility

Technology as a Tool for Community Empowerment

We strive to use technology to help empower and shape the effectiveness of communities and institutions — wherever they may be, no matter the circumstances and locations. Our outreach is delivered through educational programmes and technology-based intervention initiatives.

Giving back

A long history of giving back

ProBASE has a long history of giving back to the community, taking part in philanthropic causes and creating positive social value. Our work focuses on three areas:

  • HealthHospital wards, maternity care and children's health.
  • EducationHackathons, coding bootcamps and school visits.
  • The vulnerable & socially disadvantagedOrphaned and vulnerable children and the homes that care for them.

Zambia@60 National Hackathon

ProBASE Limited collaborated with the Zambia@60 National Committee and the National Science Centre, under the Ministry of Education, to host a national hackathon for students from across Zambia.

Educational programme

Opening Our Doors to Zambia's Next Generation

Beyond the systems we build, ProBASE invests in the people who will build tomorrow's. We hosted pupils from Great North Road Academy at our offices for a hands-on look at the technology, design and software behind the platforms we run — from live demos to conversations with the engineers and designers who build them.

It's part of our ongoing commitment to giving young Zambians early, real exposure to careers in technology — because the innovators solving tomorrow's problems are sitting in classrooms today.
