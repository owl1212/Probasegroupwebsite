Messaging Infrastructure at National Scale

A custom-built messaging platform that has delivered more than 25 million SMS, keeping institutions and the people they serve informed in real time.

The challenge

What needed to change

When someone pays a tax, a contribution or a bill, they want to know straight away that it went through. Institutions also need to reach large numbers of people quickly, whether for confirmations, reminders or service updates.

Doing that at national scale means sending millions of messages reliably, integrating with mobile networks, and keeping delivery fast even at peak times, when off-the-shelf tools often fall short.

Our solution

What ProBASE delivered

ProBASE designed and built its own messaging infrastructure, shaped around how institutions actually operate. The platform connects to mobile networks and to ProBASE's transaction systems, so messages can be triggered automatically by real events, such as a successful payment.

Because it is built in-house, the platform can be tuned for volume, reliability and the specific integrations each client needs.

How it works

From first connection to everyday operation

  1. 01

    Integrate

    The gateway connects to mobile network operators and to client and ProBASE systems.

  2. 02

    Trigger

    Messages are generated automatically from real transactions and events.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    High-volume queuing and routing keep delivery fast, even at peak load.

  4. 04

    Track

    Delivery is tracked, giving institutions visibility of what reached their customers.
