National Tax Collection on SmartPAY

How SmartPAY became the main channel through which Zambia's national taxes are paid, connecting taxpayers, banks and the revenue authority on one reliable platform.

The challenge

What needed to change

Paying tax used to mean a trip to the bank counter, paper forms and long queues, and for the revenue authority, a wait before it could confirm what had actually been paid.

As Zambia moved its tax payments onto electronic channels, starting with domestic e-payments in 2014 and the retirement of RTGS tax payments for large and medium taxpayers in 2016, the country needed a dependable link between every bank and the tax system. Each bank had its own systems, and every payment had to be matched to the right taxpayer and tax type, quickly and without error.

Our solution

What ProBASE delivered

ProBASE built SmartPAY as a single payments and collections hub that sits between the banks and the tax system. Banks connect once to SmartPAY, and SmartPAY handles validation, routing and confirmation for every payment.

Taxpayers can pay through the channel that suits them (the bank counter, internet banking or other electronic channels), and each payment is validated against the taxpayer's details before it is accepted, then confirmed back to the revenue authority.

How it works

From first connection to everyday operation

  1. 01

    Integrate

    Each commercial bank connects to SmartPAY through a standard interface, so new banks join without rebuilding anything.

  2. 02

    Validate

    Payments are checked against taxpayer and tax-type details in real time, so errors are caught before money moves.

  3. 03

    Confirm

    The revenue authority receives payment confirmation straight away, instead of waiting for paper reconciliation.

  4. 04

    Reconcile

    Collections are matched and reported end to end, giving a clear audit trail for every kwacha received.
