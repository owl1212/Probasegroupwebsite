Case Study 02 · Digitization & Automation

Fully Digital NHIMA Collections

Helping Zambia's National Health Insurance Management Authority collect every contribution digitally, from day one of a brand-new national scheme.

The challenge

What needed to change

Zambia's National Health Insurance Scheme, established under the National Health Insurance Act of 2018, gives members access to quality healthcare funded by monthly contributions from employers and employees across the country.

A new national scheme meant collecting contributions from a very large number of employers from the start, with no legacy system to build on. Manual or paper-based collection would have meant delays, errors and a heavy reconciliation burden at exactly the moment the scheme needed to earn public trust.

Our solution

What ProBASE delivered

ProBASE delivered E-NHIMA, a digital collections service built on the same proven engine that powers SmartPAY. Instead of building and testing a new payments network from scratch, NHIMA could plug straight into infrastructure that banks already trusted and used every day.

Employers pay contributions through their banks' electronic channels. Each payment is validated, matched to the right employer account and confirmed to NHIMA automatically.

How it works

From first connection to everyday operation

  1. 01

    Reuse

    E-NHIMA runs on the SmartPAY engine, so the authority started with production-grade, bank-tested infrastructure.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Participating banks were connected so employers can pay contributions through the banks they already use.

  3. 03

    Automate

    Payments are validated and matched to employer records automatically, with no manual capture.

  4. 04

    Report

    NHIMA gets clear, timely collection data to manage the scheme and serve its members.
