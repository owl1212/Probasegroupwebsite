Our story

From Lusaka to the region, one system at a time.

Starting in 2010, ProBASE has grown steadily as a strategic service partner and system integrator for the public and private sectors, with one of the largest client bases in the Zambian market.

The success of our E-Tax, E-Pensions and E-NHIMA systems carried us into countries with similar needs, including the DR Congo, Malawi and Lesotho.
