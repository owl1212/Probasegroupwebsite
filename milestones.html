2023
Best Innovative Company Award

Award · 1 May 2023

Recognized for Boosting Zambia's Economy and Society

Zambia celebrated Labour Day this past Monday — a national holiday honouring the contributions of workers to the country's economy and society, marked by parades, speeches and other events organized by the Zambia Congress of Trade Unions, Lusaka District Committee.

One company that took part in the Labour Day activities was ProBASE, a leading provider of innovative technology solutions in Zambia. Forty-six members of staff marched past — the second time the company has participated in the event — alongside a colourful band and majorettes, all dressed in ProBASE colours.

ProBASE's participation was a testament to its commitment to its employees and the broader Zambian community, showing support for workers' rights and the labour movement in Zambia. During the celebrations, the company was also recognized for its contributions to the Zambian economy and society — presented with the award for Best Innovative Company by the Minister of Labour. The award reflects ProBASE's commitment to innovation and excellence, and its dedication to making a positive impact on the Zambian community.

Source: Techtrends Zambia, 3 May 2023

TCIB
Certified Participant

Certification

Certified for Africa's Homegrown Real-Time Payments Scheme

ProBASE was recognised as a TCIB Certified Participant — accreditation under Transactions Cleared on an Immediate Basis, BankservAfrica's homegrown, interoperable real-time payments scheme connecting banks and non-bank participants across the continent.

The certification reflects ProBASE's proven ability to meet the scheme's end-to-end regulatory, security and interoperability standards — reinforcing our role in building the payment infrastructure moving Africa toward faster, more inclusive, and more sustainable cross-border transactions.
