What we do

Four capabilities.
One dependable partner.

From the moment a payment is made to the software running quietly behind it — every service is built to keep critical systems accurate, reconciled and running at scale.

Service 01

Payments & Collections

Payments and collections are vital for businesses, involving fund transfers between parties through various channels like cards and digital platforms. We design and run the rails that move money reliably, at volume.

  • Card & digital payment channels

    Reliable acceptance across the channels your customers already use.

  • Bank & SmartPAY integration

    Connected to 17 of 18 banks through our SmartPAY collections platform.

  • Institutional collections

    Powers national programmes, including health insurance and tax collection.

Banks integrated with SmartPAY

Service 02

Digitization & Process Automation

Digitization is the process of converting analog or physical information into digital format — making it accessible and manipulable electronically. We turn manual, paper-based processes into workflows your team can trust.

  • Manual-to-digital workflow design

    Mapping existing processes before automating them, not just digitizing paperwork.

  • SMS & messaging infrastructure

    Platforms handling over 25 million SMS transactions and counting.

  • Accessible, auditable records

    Digital records that are searchable, reportable and easy to hand to auditors.

25M+
SMS transactions processed

Service 03

Transaction Processing, Analytics & Reconciliation

Transaction processing involves the systematic recording, processing and completion of financial transactions — ensuring accuracy and efficiency in managing financial data at every step.

  • High-volume reconciliation

    Built to handle national-scale volumes without losing a single record.

  • E-toll & usage-based transactions

    Processing infrastructure behind more than 30 million e-toll transactions.

  • Reporting & analytics

    Clear visibility into transaction flows for finance and operations teams.

30M+
E-toll transactions handled

Service 04

Bespoke Software Development

Bespoke software development is the creation of customized software solutions, tailored to specific business requirements and objectives — designed around the customer, not a generic template.

  • Custom-built, not off-the-shelf

    Software shaped around how your business actually operates.

  • Built by the SmartPAY team

    The same engineering discipline behind our national payments platforms.

  • Long-term support

    Software maintained and evolved alongside your business, not abandoned at launch.
